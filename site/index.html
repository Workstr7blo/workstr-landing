How it works

One loop: log, recover, progress, own.

Workstr is designed around the training decisions you make every week, not around social-feed noise.

01

Log the session

Record sets, reps, weight, notes, and body context without account friction.

02

Read readiness

Completed sets update the recovery view so fatigue is visible before the next session.

03

Review progress

Dashboards turn sessions into volume, consistency, and muscle-distribution signals.

04

Keep the record

Your history stays local-first and portable, with optional Nostr publishing when useful.
