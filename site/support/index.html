What zaps fund

Why 85,000 sats per month?

Workstr's support target is not a random fundraiser number. It is the monthly equivalent of current operating costs, rounded so verified zap receipts can be compared to the burn in sats.

Monthly target 85,000 sats

Keeps Workstr operating and moving while zaps remain fully receipt-auditable.
