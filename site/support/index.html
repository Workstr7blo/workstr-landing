Transparent accounting

50,000 sats / month

  • The app meter queries public zap relays for this month's receipts.
  • Receipts must be tagged to the Workstr operator pubkey and signed by the wallet provider.
  • If relays are unreachable, the meter says unknown instead of pretending the total is zero.
