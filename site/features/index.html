Log/Recover/Own

Features that keep training simple.

Workstr gives you the essentials: log sessions fast, understand recovery, track progress, and keep the proof without account lock-in.

01 / Logging

Fast workout records

Capture exercises, sets, reps, load, and notes quickly enough for the gym floor.

02 / Recovery

Muscle readiness map

See what was trained recently and what is ready before planning the next session.

03 / Progress

Simple training signals

Review streaks, volume, session history, and muscle distribution without spreadsheets.

04 / Library

Reusable exercises

Build from movements and programs that can travel with your training history.

05 / Ownership

Local-first data

No mandatory account. Your log stays yours, with export and import for portability.

06 / Nostr

Optional public proof

Keep training private by default, or publish proof and support the build with zap receipts.

Bottom line

Train hard. Keep the proof.

Workstr is focused on the core loop: record the work, read the recovery picture, and keep your training history portable.
