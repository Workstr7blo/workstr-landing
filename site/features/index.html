Fast logging

Record the work before the pump fades.

Workstr is built for the moment you are actually training. Add sets, reps, load, notes, and session details without fighting a signup wall or slow app flow.

What it does
Turns each exercise and set into a structured training record.
Why it matters
You can look back at real work, not guesses from memory.
How it feels
Quick enough for the gym floor, clear enough for later review.
Session record Exercise, sets, reps, load, notes.

Simple inputs become a training history you can reuse, analyze, export, and publish when you want public proof.

Recovery map

See what is ready before you train it again.

Every logged set feeds a recovery view. Muscles move from worked to rebuilding to ready, giving you a quick way to choose the next session without overthinking.

What it does
Translates recent training into muscle readiness signals.
Why it matters
Hard training works better when fatigue is visible.
Useful for
Full-body sessions, stabilizer-heavy tools, and planning around sore muscle groups.
Progress dashboard

Make consistency visible.

The dashboard turns sessions into signals: streaks, total sessions, volume, recent activity, and muscle distribution. It shows whether the work is actually compounding.

What it does
Summarizes your training history into practical metrics.
Why it matters
Progress is easier to protect when you can see it.
Best use
Review the week, spot gaps, and decide what should happen next.
Body metrics

Keep body trends beside training history.

Strength, recovery, weight, and goals are connected. Workstr keeps body metrics near the training log so you can read progress without scattering the story across apps.

What it does
Tracks body measures and goal movement over time.
Why it matters
Training context matters when weight or body-composition goals change.
Privacy angle
Sensitive body records stay part of your local-first log.
Exercise library

Build sessions from reusable movements.

The library gives exercises and programs a home. Movements can carry photos, target muscles, difficulty, and import actions so a training block can be rebuilt instead of rewritten.

What it does
Organizes exercises and program building blocks.
Why it matters
Reusable structure saves time and keeps plans consistent.
Designed for
Strength training, bodyweight work, kettlebells, mace, club, and custom movements.
Local-first ownership

No account gate. No rented progress.

Workstr starts from the idea that your training history should belong to you. The app is usable without mandatory login, stores your log locally, and supports portability through export and import.

What it does
Keeps the core workout log local-first and portable.
Why it matters
You should not lose years of training because a service changes terms or disappears.
Plain English

Optional Nostr publishing
